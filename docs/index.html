Arcade1870 (ARC) rewards

Winning matches issues a signed claim against the shared, pre-funded Arcade1870RewardVault smart contract - the exact same vault and ARC token used by Crypto Chess. Connect a MetaMask-compatible wallet in-game, win a match, and submit the signed claim to receive ARC directly in your wallet. The vault only validates a signed claim and doesn't care which game requested it, so the same treasury can safely be shared across multiple Arcade1870 games.

  • Token: Arcade1870 (ARC), an ERC-20 on Ethereum Mainnet
  • Reward contract: Arcade1870RewardVault (EIP-712 signed claims)
  • Players pay their own gas and receive ARC directly - the backend never holds player funds

System requirements

  • OS: Windows 10 or later (64-bit)
  • A MetaMask-compatible Ethereum wallet to receive ARC rewards (optional, only needed to claim rewards)
